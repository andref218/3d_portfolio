# Personal Interests

Outside of software development, André enjoys activities that encourage creativity, strategic thinking, and continuous learning.

## Basketball

Basketball is one of André's biggest passions outside of software development. He enjoys playing the sport and following it closely, appreciating its competitive, strategic, and team-oriented nature.

## Video Games

André enjoys playing video games, especially story-driven games with immersive worlds and memorable characters. He appreciates how games combine technology, design, storytelling, and creativity, and often finds inspiration in the way they are built.

## Vinyl Records

Music has always been one of André's passions, and he enjoys collecting and discovering vinyl records. This interest inspired him to build **Vynilz**, a full-stack web application designed to help collectors manage, explore, and showcase their vinyl collections.
